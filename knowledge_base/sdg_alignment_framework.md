# UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment Framework

## Primary Focus: SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy
- **Target 7.2:** Increase substantially the share of renewable energy in the global energy mix.
- **Target 7.3:** Double the global rate of improvement in energy efficiency.
- **EcoSync Implementation:**
  - Continuous tracking of Energy Use Intensity (EUI in kWh/m² or kWh/occupant).
  - Identification and elimination of off-hours parasitic baseline loads.
  - Granular peak load shaving to reduce reliance on carbon-intensive peaker power plants.
  - Conversion of avoided kilowatt-hours directly into verified scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions reductions (tCO2e).

## Secondary Focus: SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation
- **Target 6.4:** Substantially increase water-use efficiency across all sectors and ensure sustainable withdrawals and supply of freshwater.
- **EcoSync Implementation:**
  - Automated Minimum Night Flow (MNF) monitoring to detect plumbing ruptures within 2 hours instead of monthly utility billing cycles.
  - Tracking water flow intensity across specialized facility archetypes (research labs, dining halls, academic halls).
  - Rapid dispatch triage workflows preventing thousands of cubic meters of treated potable water loss.

## Secondary Focus: SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production
- **Target 12.5:** Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling, and reuse.
- **EcoSync Implementation:**
  - Multi-stream material tracking (landfill, single-stream recyclables, compostable organics).
  - Diversion rate computation and trend tracking against zero-waste benchmarks.
  - Early-warning detection of recycling contamination and source reduction impact modeling.

## Responsible AI & Transparency Principles
- **Explainability:** All AI diagnostic suggestions are framed as *hypotheses* (e.g., "Possible explanation") backed by statistical confidence scores, not definitive claims.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** EcoSync empowers human facility engineers and sustainability officers to make informed operational decisions rather than taking autonomous irreversible physical actions.
- **Grounded Provenance:** Every Copilot answer is linked to either quantitative database telemetry or established engineering standards (ASHRAE, EPA, LEED, UN SDG).
