# Campus & Commercial Energy Efficiency Guidelines (SDG 7 Alignment)

## 1. ASHRAE Standard 90.1 & Setback Guidelines
- **Unoccupied Temperature Setbacks:** During non-business hours (typically 20:00 to 06:00 and weekends), thermostat heating setpoints should be reduced to 16°C (60°F) and cooling setpoints increased to 28°C (82°F). This single operational modification reduces overnight HVAC energy consumption by 15% to 28%.
- **Baseload Management:** Unoccupied baseload should not exceed 25-30% of peak daytime operational demand. If baseload exceeds 45%, investigate continuous ventilation fans running at 100%, un-scheduled chilled water circulation, or computing labs left fully energized.

## 2. Peak Demand Shaving & Load Balancing
- **Staggered Startups:** Large mechanical equipment (chillers, air handling units, industrial air compressors) should initiate sequentially with 10-15 minute offsets to avoid utility peak demand charges.
- **Pre-Cooling Strategies:** In extreme weather, pre-cool academic and administrative buildings by 1.5°C during early morning off-peak hours (04:00 - 07:00), allowing cooling curtailment during peak tariff hours (14:00 - 18:00).
- **Demand Response Automation:** Auto-shed non-critical auxiliary lighting and plug loads when building demand crosses 90% of monthly contract capacity.

## 3. Lighting & Plug Load Controls
- **Occupancy Sensor Auditing:** PIR and ultrasonic occupancy sensors must timeout within 15 minutes of inactivity in classrooms, lecture halls, and storage rooms.
- **Smart Power Strips:** Deploy smart power distribution units (PDUs) in computer labs to eliminate phantom loads (vampire draw) from peripherals and idle workstations overnight.

## 4. Diagnostics for Energy Anomalies
- **Symptom: High weekend consumption in Academic/Office buildings.**
  - *Probable Root Cause:* HVAC schedule override manually engaged by cleaning staff or events, but not reverted.
  - *Recommended Action:* Check Building Automation System (BAS) schedule overrides; inspect zone dampers and variable air volume (VAV) boxes.
- **Symptom: Sharp spike in lab facilities (Building B).**
  - *Probable Root Cause:* Laboratory fume hood sash left open, driving 100% outside air exhaust fans at maximum velocity.
  - *Recommended Action:* Verify sash closure and variable air volume exhaust controllers.
